1｜先準備這些資料

  • 臺灣官方用藥紀錄或目前處方。
  • 身心科診所的藥袋或用藥紀錄。

先準備上面兩類即可。若醫師需要其他資料，再依要求補上；不要寄送與看診無關的身分資料。

2｜開啟 Well Revolution

佳蓉沒有 GP，可以直接向 Well Revolution 的紐西蘭 GP 開啟線上問診，不必先加入診所。問診可用文字傳訊息與上傳文件。

開啟 Prescription Renewal 查看費用

一般時段：每天上午 8 點至下午 6 點，NZ$50。其他時段及國定假日：NZ$80。以上為官方目前標示價格。

3｜複製這段給醫師

先開文字問診，再整段貼上。醫師追問時，再照實回答。

Hello Doctor, I am in Christchurch, New Zealand, on a Working Holiday visa. I do not have a GP. I currently take escitalopram 10 mg, one tablet once a day. This is ongoing treatment that started in Taiwan. I would like you to review whether it is suitable to continue. I can upload my medical records and medication details. My clinic medication bag dated 17 June 2026 shows that I was taking 20 mg a day at that time. My current dose is 10 mg a day. I find spoken English difficult. Could we communicate by written chat? If you think a prescription is appropriate, could you send it to Chemist Warehouse Westfield Riccarton for collection? Thank you.
看中文意思

醫師您好，我目前持 Working Holiday visa，在紐西蘭基督城，沒有 GP。

我現在每天服用 escitalopram 10 mg 一錠，是從臺灣延續的治療。我想請您評估是否適合繼續使用。

我可以上傳用藥紀錄。2026 年 6 月 17 日的診所藥袋顯示我當時每天服用 20 mg；目前劑量是每天 10 mg。

我不擅長口語英文，能否用文字問診？如果您認為適合開處方，能否送到 Chemist Warehouse Westfield Riccarton 讓我領取？

請如實回答醫師對症狀、效果、副作用、其他藥物及過敏的提問。不要自行改藥量；由醫師決定是否續方。

醫師可能追問｜中英 Q&A

點開看常見問題與回答提示

先把方括號改成佳蓉的實際情況，再複製英文回答。若不確定，就直接說不確定，不要猜；藥名、劑量及處方是否適合，由醫師核對與評估。

Doctor: When did you start taking escitalopram, and what was it prescribed for?

醫師：你何時開始服用 escitalopram？當初是為了什麼原因開立？

可回答（填入括號）：

I started taking escitalopram in Taiwan around [month/year]. My original clinician prescribed it for [reason or diagnosis, if known]. I can upload my medical records.

我大約在［年月］於臺灣開始服用。原醫師告訴我用途是［原因或診斷；若知道再填］。我可以上傳用藥紀錄。

Doctor: What dose are you taking now? Has it changed?

醫師：你現在服用多少？劑量曾經改變嗎？

可回答（填入括號；不確定就照實說）：

I currently take escitalopram 10 mg once daily. My clinic medication bag dated 17 June 2026 shows 20 mg per day at that time. My dose changed to 10 mg around [date; if unsure, say you are not sure]. [If known, explain who advised the change.] I can upload my records.

我目前每天服用 escitalopram 10 mg。2026 年 6 月 17 日的診所藥袋記錄當時為每天 20 mg；後來約在［日期；不確定就說不確定］改為 10 mg。［若知道，說明是誰建議調整。］我可以上傳紀錄。

Doctor: How is it helping, and what symptoms do you have now?

醫師：這個藥對你有幫助嗎？你目前有哪些症狀？

可回答（只描述真實感受）：

At present, I feel [describe your current symptoms]. Escitalopram has [helped / not helped / I am not sure] with [symptom]. It affects my daily life by [brief example, if relevant].

我目前感受到［描述症狀］。Escitalopram 對［症狀］［有幫助／沒有幫助／不確定］；目前影響生活的情況是［簡短例子；若適用］。

Doctor: Have you had any side effects or other problems?

醫師：你有副作用或其他不舒服嗎？

可回答（擇一並補上真實情況）：

I have had [side effect], which started around [date] and affects me by [brief description]. / I am not aware of any side effects.

我有［副作用］，大約從［日期］開始，影響是［簡短說明］。／我目前沒有注意到副作用。

Doctor: What other medicines or supplements do you take? Do you have allergies or other health conditions?

醫師：你還有服用哪些藥物或保健品？有過敏或其他健康狀況嗎？

可回答（列出全部，沒有就照實寫 none）：

Besides escitalopram, I take [medicine or supplement, strength and how often]. My known allergies are [list them / none that I know of]. I also have [other health conditions / none that I know of].

除了 escitalopram，我還服用［藥物或保健品、劑量及頻率］。我知道的過敏是［列出／目前沒有已知過敏］；其他健康狀況是［列出／目前沒有已知狀況］。

Doctor: Have you missed any doses or run out recently?

醫師：最近有漏服或藥吃完的情況嗎？

可回答（依實際情況填寫）：

I [have / have not] missed any doses recently. My last dose was [date/time], and I have about [number] tablets left.

我最近［有／沒有］漏服。最近一次服藥是［日期／時間］，目前大約還剩［數量］錠。

Doctor: Have you had thoughts of harming yourself or felt unsafe?

醫師：你有過傷害自己的念頭，或覺得自己不安全嗎？

請誠實回答，不要套用不符合自己的答案：

I [have / have not] had thoughts of harming myself or suicide. [If yes, explain when this happened and whether I feel in immediate danger now.]

我［有／沒有］過傷害自己或自殺的念頭。［若有，說明何時發生，以及目前是否有立即危險。］

若目前有立即危險，請撥紐西蘭 111；需要找人談談時，可免費電話或簡訊 1737。Health NZ：緊急求助｜Health NZ：1737 支援

4｜藥師與藥局：照順序詢價、送處方、取藥

等 Well Revolution 醫師完成評估，並確認電子處方已開出後，再處理藥局。不要先下單或付款。

指定門市：Chemist Warehouse at Westfield Riccarton，129 Riccarton Road, Christchurch 8041。門市位於 Level 1、Rotherham Street 外側，靠近 Drexels；電話：03 667 0901。

  1. 先詢問：確認這張電子處方能否在指定門市取貨、正確提交方式，以及 28 錠和 84 錠各自的總價。
  2. 問清費用：請藥局列出藥品、調劑／私人處方費，以及每次 repeat 調劑費；未確認補助資格前，不要把補助價當作自己的實付價。
  3. 再送處方：依藥局確認的流程操作。Chemist Warehouse 官方說明：電子處方先在線上完成訂單，再將 eScript 照片和訂單號寄到處方頁所列的 Email。若結帳時沒有 Westfield Riccarton 取貨選項，先停下來詢問，不要改選配送或付款。
  4. 等通知：只在收到藥局文字或 Email 確認「可以取藥」後出發。帶訂單確認資料、可接受的實體照片身分證件；若下單時使用信用卡，也帶該卡以備核對。

藥局可能會問什麼｜中英 Q&A

Pharmacy: Are you eligible for funded medicines in New Zealand?

藥局：你符合紐西蘭補助藥品資格嗎？

可回答：I am in New Zealand on a Working Holiday visa, and I am not sure whether I qualify for funded medicines. Could you please confirm, or quote the private cash price if you cannot confirm my eligibility?

我持紐西蘭 Working Holiday visa，不確定是否符合補助資格。請協助確認；若無法確認，請提供私費總價。

Pharmacy: Do you have a New Zealand prescription or ePrescription yet?

藥局：你已經有紐西蘭處方或電子處方了嗎？

已開出時：Yes. My Well Revolution doctor has issued an ePrescription. Please tell me how to submit it for collection at Westfield Riccarton.

有。Well Revolution 醫師已開出電子處方。請告訴我如何提交，並在 Westfield Riccarton 取藥。

尚未開出時：Not yet. I am waiting for my doctor to assess my request. I will contact you after the ePrescription is issued.

還沒有。我正在等醫師評估；電子處方開出後再聯絡你們。

Pharmacy: What medicine, dose and quantity are on the prescription?

藥局：處方上的藥名、劑量和數量是什麼？

可回答：It is escitalopram 10 mg, one tablet once daily. Please follow the medicine and quantity authorised on my ePrescription. I accept a generic brand if it is appropriate for this prescription.

是 escitalopram 10 mg，每日一次、每次一錠。請依電子處方上的藥品和數量調劑；若處方適用，我可以接受學名藥。

Pharmacy: Which store would you like to collect it from?

藥局：你想在哪一家門市取藥？

可回答：Chemist Warehouse at Westfield Riccarton, 129 Riccarton Road, Christchurch, please.

請安排到 Westfield Riccarton 的 Chemist Warehouse，地址是 129 Riccarton Road, Christchurch。

Pharmacy: The quoted price does not include a dispensing or private-prescription fee.

藥局：目前報價還不包含調劑費或私人處方費。

可回答：Thank you. Could you please tell me the final total, including every fee, before I place the order?

謝謝。下單前，請告訴我包含所有費用後的最終總額。

Pharmacy: We can only dispense 28 tablets today / this quantity is not in stock.

藥局：今天只能調劑 28 錠／目前沒有足夠庫存。

可回答：Please dispense only the quantity authorised by my prescriber. Could you tell me whether the remaining authorised quantity can be collected later, when it may be ready, and whether another fee applies?

請只調劑醫師處方授權的數量。請告訴我其餘已授權的數量能否稍後領取、預計何時備妥，以及是否會再收費。

Pharmacy: How do the repeats on your prescription work?

藥局：你的處方有 repeats（續領次數），要怎麼使用？

可回答：Could you please tell me how many repeats are authorised, when each repeat can be dispensed, the fee for each repeat, and whether you can send me a reminder?

請告訴我處方授權幾次續領、每次何時可以調劑、是否另收費，以及能否在下次可領時提醒我。

Pharmacy: When will you collect it?

藥局：你什麼時候來取藥？

可回答：Please let me know by text or email when it is ready. I will come to Westfield Riccarton after I receive your confirmation.

請用簡訊或 Email 通知我藥已備妥；收到確認後，我再到 Westfield Riccarton。

藥師可能會問什麼｜中英 Q&A

Pharmacist: May I see your photo ID and order confirmation?

藥師：可以看一下你的照片身分證件和訂單確認嗎？

可回答：Yes. Here is my physical photo ID and order confirmation.

可以。這是我的實體照片身分證件和訂單確認資料。

Pharmacist: Are you taking any other medicines or supplements? Do you have any allergies?

藥師：你還有服用其他藥物或保健品嗎？有沒有過敏？

請換成真實情況：I also take [list all medicines and supplements]. My known allergies are [list them / none that I know of].

我也有服用［列出所有藥物及保健品］。我知道的過敏是［列出／目前沒有已知過敏］。

Pharmacist: Would you like me to explain how to use this medicine?

藥師：需要我說明這個藥怎麼使用嗎？

可回答：Yes, please. I find spoken English difficult. Could you explain in writing how to follow the label, what to do if I miss a dose, the important side effects or interactions, and who I should contact if I have a concern?

需要，謝謝。我不擅長口語英文。請用文字說明如何依藥袋指示服用、漏服時怎麼辦、重要副作用或交互作用，以及有疑問時該聯絡誰。

如果藥名、劑量、藥袋指示或價格和預期不同，先請藥師用文字說明；不要自行改劑量、換藥或多領數量。任何不確定處，請藥師先向開方醫師確認。
